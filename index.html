Privacy PolicyTerms & Conditions

1. Who We Are

GoalPilot is a productivity and habit-tracking application published by Zonix Tech Solutions. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact info@zonixtechsolutions.com.

2. Information Processed

GoalPilot allows you to create task titles, descriptions, routines, priorities, goals, categories, due dates, and completion histories. These items, together with streaks and progress reports, are intended to be stored on your device. If you choose to use the AI Assistant, the text you submit or text produced from voice input is transmitted for AI processing. When you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Network and third-party services may also process technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata.

3. Why We Process Information

We process information to display and organize your items, calculate progress and streaks, deliver the reminders you configure, handle support requests, and, when requested, generate or organize tasks and goals through AI. The core planner is designed to function without requiring an account.

4. AI Assistant and Google Gemini

If you use the optional AI Assistant, your submitted prompt, including text generated from speech, is sent to Google Gemini to produce the requested suggestion. The provider may process or retain requests, responses, and technical data according to the terms applicable to the API service and account configuration in use. Google describes different practices for unpaid and paid Gemini API services; unpaid-service content may be used to improve its services and may be human-reviewed, while paid services have different restrictions and limited safety-related retention. Do not include sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information in AI prompts. Please review Google’s Gemini API terms for details: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

5. Voice Input and Device Permissions

Microphone access is requested when you start voice input. Speech may be converted to text using device or platform speech-recognition functionality; depending on the provider and device configuration, this may involve separate processing by that provider. Bluetooth-related permissions, where requested, are used to support compatible audio devices. Notification permission is used for reminders you enable. You can manage applicable permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may make the related feature unavailable without preventing access to other features.

6. Storage, Sharing, and Security

GoalPilot is designed to store your planner items on your device rather than in a GoalPilot user account. The app transmits optional AI requests to Google Gemini and may rely on device or platform services for voice recognition and notifications. We do not sell user data. Network transmissions should use encrypted HTTPS connections; the app’s production configuration must be verified to confirm this. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual planner items through the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally removes app data stored locally on the device; however, device backups or operating-system mechanisms may retain copies depending on your settings. Deleting local data does not automatically delete AI requests or technical records previously processed by third parties. For questions concerning provider processing or privacy requests, contact info@zonixtechsolutions.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

GoalPilot is not intended for children under 13 or any higher minimum age applicable in their location. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children below the applicable age. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the app, contact us.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You may review, edit, and delete locally stored planner items in the app and manage permissions through your device settings. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy choices relating to personal data processed by us. Email info@zonixtechsolutions.com and we will review your request as required by applicable law.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as our features, service providers, or legal obligations change. We will revise the “Last updated” date and, when required, provide additional notice.
